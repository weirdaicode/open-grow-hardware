ESP32-2432S028R · 320 × 240 · firmware 3.3

A grow-tent gauge on a $15 screen

This is the LogMyGrow Display firmware running in your browser on made-up tents. Tap the tabs along the bottom of the screen, swipe, or press BOOT the way you would on the board. Leave it alone for 20 seconds and it drops to the climate dial.

Try a scenario

The simulated day runs in 96 seconds: lights on, the afternoon peak, lights off and the humid night. Every number on the screen is computed from that model, not canned.

What you're looking at

Home
One card per space. Temperature, RH and VPD each take the colour of their own tier for the plants' stage.
Space
VPD large, a band bar per reading (yellow is OK, green is ideal), the top two things to do.
To do
The most urgent actions across every space.
Plugs
Plug buttons. Off until the grower allows it in the app.
Dial
Two crossed needles. Where they meet is your air, drawn over the ideal box, the mold zone and VPD curves.

Two firmwares

The full one reads stage targets, mold risk and to-dos from LogMyGrow. The ESPHome one needs no account: it shows two Home Assistant sensors and works out VPD on the device.

No cables after day one

Pair by a 6-letter code. Updates arrive over the air into the second app slot, checked against their sha256 before the board switches over.

Open and self-hostable

MIT firmware. The server address is a setting in the Wi-Fi portal, so the display can point at your own LogMyGrow server.

Simulation of firmware 3.3 screens with example tents named Flower Tent and Veg Tent. Real displays show your own spaces. Colours on a real panel are less saturated than on your monitor.
